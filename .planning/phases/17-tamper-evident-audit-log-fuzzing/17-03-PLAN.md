<objective>
Implement the three native Go fuzz targets (FuzzHMACVerify, FuzzConfigLoad, FuzzHuJSONParse) with seed corpora, gitleaks pre-commit hook for testdata secret scanning, and fuzz.yml CI workflow.

Purpose: Fuzzing provides property-based assurance that the parsers most likely to encounter untrusted input (config YAML, HuJSON ACL, HMAC verify) fail closed — returning errors, never panicking — on adversarial input. The gitleaks hook and CI job prevent real secrets from accumulating in the seed corpus. This plan runs after 17-02 so the AuditWriter interface refactor is complete and the build is stable before fuzz test files reference internal/audit/signed.go package-private symbols.

Output: Three fuzz test files, 12 corpus files (4 per target), .githooks/pre-commit, .github/workflows/fuzz.yml.
</objective>
